What is unsays?

unsays is a free multiplayer acronym party game that runs in your browser. Everyone at the table gets the same random letters, like B-O-I-Q, and writes a phrase whose words start with them: "Bonus or I quit." The lines are shown without names, everyone votes for the funniest, and the round winner picks the next topic. It's a modern take on Acrophobia, the 1990s acronym game.

Fast rounds

Turbo tables give 30 seconds to write and 15 to vote. First to 30 points plays a 3-round face-off. Full rules
